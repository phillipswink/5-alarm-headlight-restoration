Frequently Asked Questions

What customers want to know.

Do you come to the customer?

Yes. 5-Alarm is a mobile service and can come to a home, workplace, dealership or shop within the service area.

Can every headlight be restored?

Many headlights with exterior haze, oxidation and yellowing can be improved. Internal damage, cracks, moisture intrusion or failing components may require repair or replacement.

How do I request a quote?

Call, text or use the form below. Include your vehicle year, make and model, your location and clear photos of both headlights.

Do you work with dealerships and repair shops?

Yes. Contact us to discuss recurring or multi-vehicle service for dealerships, shops and small fleets.
