Our promise

If a qualifying restored headlight develops premature yellowing, exterior oxidation, peeling or ceramic UV coating failure under normal environmental exposure within three years of the service date, contact us. We will inspect the lens and, when the condition is covered, re-restore the affected headlight at no charge for labor or restoration materials.

This warranty applies to the quality and durability of the exterior restoration performed by 5-Alarm Headlight Restoration. It is not a warranty on the headlight assembly, bulb, wiring or vehicle.

What is covered

  • Premature exterior yellowing caused by normal UV exposure
  • Return of exterior surface oxidation
  • Peeling, flaking or failure of the applied protective coating
  • Significant loss of clarity caused by failure of our restoration process
  • Labor and restoration materials for an approved warranty correction

What is not covered

  • Cracks, broken lenses, rock chips, impacts or collision damage
  • Internal haze, condensation, water intrusion or failed seals
  • Damage from fire, vandalism, pressure washing, abrasive cleaners or chemicals
  • Normal scratches, road wear or damage unrelated to coating failure
  • Bulbs, wiring, housings, seals, electronics or mechanical components
  • Work altered, polished, coated or repaired by another person or company

Warranty requirements

  1. Proof of service: Keep the original invoice or receipt showing the service date and vehicle.
  2. Original vehicle: Coverage stays with the serviced vehicle and is available to the original purchasing customer unless otherwise stated in writing.
  3. Prompt notice: Contact us when you first notice a possible covered condition and provide clear photos.
  4. Inspection: We must be allowed to inspect the headlight before warranty work is approved.
  5. Service area: Mobile warranty service is subject to our current North Texas service area. A mutually agreed location may be required.

Care recommendations

Wash headlights with normal automotive soap and a soft wash mitt or microfiber towel. Avoid abrasive compounds, harsh solvents and aggressive pressure washing directly at lens edges. Proper care helps the ceramic UV protection perform as intended.

How to request warranty service

Text 469-826-0859 or email info@5alarmheadlightrestoration.com with your name, vehicle year/make/model, original service date and clear photos of the affected headlight.
